About Us

Our Mission

At Top Score Tuition Centre, we are dedicated to empowering learners across Botswana by providing tailored academic support for IGCSE, BGCSE, JCE, and PSLE. Founded with the belief that every student deserves the chance to excel, we combine experienced tutors, proven teaching strategies, and inclusive learning methods to meet diverse needs. Our team of qualified educators brings subject expertise and a passion for guiding students toward success, whether through one-on-one sessions, group learning, exam preparation, or special needs tuition. With a focus on building confidence, improving performance, and nurturing lifelong skills, we have proudly helped countless learners achieve outstanding results and unlock their full potential.

History & Background

Top Score Tuition Centre was established in 2020 with a clear vision: to provide high-quality, affordable, and inclusive academic support for learners across Botswana. What began as a small initiative to help a handful of students prepare for their exams has grown into a trusted tuition hub serving learners at every stage — from PSLE and JCE to BGCSE and IGCSE. Over the years, we have built a reputation for combining experienced tutors, modern teaching methods, and a supportive environment that nurtures confidence and results. Our journey reflects a commitment to excellence, community impact, and the belief that every student deserves the tools to succeed academically and beyond.
